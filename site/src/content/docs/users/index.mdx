Logbook tracks the time you spend coding, with or without AI. A small agent runs in the background on your computer. You install it once, and after that there is nothing to start or stop.

It keeps two numbers for you:

- **At the keyboard**: the time your editor plugins logged, the same kind of number Hackatime and WakaTime show.
- **Counted**: the minutes you were actually working, after Logbook takes off idle time and time an AI agent ran while you were away. Every cut says why.

When you build for a program, you send your counted hours with `logbook submit`. The program gets a review link that shows how the hours were counted.
