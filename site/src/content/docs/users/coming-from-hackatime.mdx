If you use Hackatime or WakaTime today, keep your editor plugins. Logbook works with them, keeps sending your time to Hackatime, and can bring your old history over.

## Your plugins keep working

Hackatime and WakaTime plugins read one file, `.wakatime.cfg` in your home folder. The installer changes only two lines in it, `api_url` and `api_key`, so they point at the Logbook agent on your computer. Every other line stays as it was, and the original is backed up.

You don't install anything new in your editor. Restart it after installing Logbook.

## Hackatime still gets your time (mirroring)

The agent sends each heartbeat to Logbook and a copy to wherever your `.wakatime.cfg` pointed before:

| Before Logbook, your config pointed at | Copies go to |
|---|---|
| Hackatime | Hackatime |
| WakaTime, or no `api_url` | WakaTime |
| another WakaTime-compatible server | that server |
| no key at all | nowhere (mirroring is off) |

Your old key is kept only on your computer and is never printed. Programs that still read hours from Hackatime keep working.

Check it with `logbook status`: the `Hackatime:` line says where copies go. The install output also has a `mirror` line.

<Aside>
To stop mirroring, run `logbook install --key YOUR_KEY --no-mirror`. Keys are shown only once, so get a new one in **Settings**, under **Install keys**. Your editors then send only to Logbook. The output prints the command to turn mirroring back on.
</Aside>

## Bring your Hackatime history

<Steps>

1. On [logbook.boogles.dev](https://logbook.boogles.dev), open **Settings** and find the **Hackatime** section.

   <Shot name="web-settings-hackatime.png" alt="The Hackatime section of Settings with the import progress" />

2. Choose **Link Hackatime** and allow access on Hackatime.

3. Wait for the import. The last 30 days come first, usually within seconds, then older history fills in.

</Steps>

How to tell it worked: the Hackatime section says how many heartbeats were imported and from which months, and your dashboard shows your past days.

## What's different

- **Imported history is "at the keyboard" time only.** Logbook can only count time from now on, because counting needs the agent on your computer. Old Hackatime time is never shown as counted. See [What counts](/users/what-counts/).
- **Counted time is usually lower than Hackatime's number.** Hackatime counts time at the keyboard. Logbook also takes off idle time and time an AI agent ran while you were away.
- **Your Hackatime trust level carries over.** If your Hackatime account is banned, you can't submit hours with Logbook either. You can submit without linking Hackatime; the review page then says it isn't linked.
- **One Hackatime account links to one Logbook account.**

## Going back

`logbook uninstall` puts your `.wakatime.cfg` back the way it was, so your plugins send straight to Hackatime again. See [Uninstall](/users/uninstall/).
