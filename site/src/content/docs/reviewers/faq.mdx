---
title: FAQ
description: Short answers to the questions reviewers ask most about Logbook review pages.
---

## Do I need a Logbook account to read a review link?

No. Anyone with the link can read the page. You need to sign in only to record a decision, and only a reviewer for the submission's program can. See [Open a review link](/reviewers/open-a-review-link/).

## Do I need to install Logbook to review?

No. Reviewing happens in the browser. Signing in with Hack Club is enough.

## Why can't I see the Approve, Adjust and Reject buttons?

Either you aren't signed in, you aren't a reviewer for this program yet, or you submitted it yourself. Ask the program's owner to add your Logbook username as a reviewer.

## Is counted time the same as Hackatime time?

No. Hackatime's figure is closer to **at the keyboard**. Counted time starts from when the submitter was actually present and working, and removes idle time, time an AI agent worked alone, and a few other patterns. See [Read the hours](/reviewers/read-the-hours/).

## Does Logbook apply the one-third AI rule for me?

No. The page shows what the one-third rule would give, next to counted time, so you can compare. Which number your program accepts is up to your program.

## Can the submitter fake the numbers?

Their computer signs its logs each day. When they submit, Logbook's server replays those logs with the public rules and checks every minute. Minutes that don't match are marked **differ**, and minutes with no logs yet are **unbacked**. See [Evidence and trust](/reviewers/evidence-and-trust/).

## Can I see the submitter's code or prompts?

No. Logbook never sends prompts, code, keystrokes or file contents to its server. You see times, counts and commit summaries. To see what was built, look at the project.

## Why are times in UTC?

Reviewers and submitters live in different time zones. UTC means everyone reads the same days. The submission's dates are UTC days too.

## What does a flag mean? Should I reject flagged hours?

A flag took nothing off. It marks minutes with an unusual pattern, such as input timing too regular for a person. Look at the flagged times on the day strips before you decide. Your program's rules decide what to do.

## I approved by mistake. Can I change it?

Yes. Press **Record a new decision** under the stamp and decide again. The newest decision counts. See [Decide](/reviewers/decide/#change-a-decision).

## Can I adjust hours up?

No. Adjusted hours must be lower than the counted hours. To accept all of them, approve.

## Why does the decision panel tell me to reload?

The submitter's other computer sent more evidence after you opened the page, so the numbers may have changed. Reload, read again, then decide.

## The link says the submission was withdrawn. What now?

The submitter withdrew it before anyone decided. Nothing is left to review. If they submit again, the new submission has a new link.

## How long does a review link work?

The page and decisions stay while the submitter's account exists. The detailed logs behind it are kept for a year after the latest decision. The SUBMISSION panel shows the date.

## TRUST says Hackatime was "not linked". Is that allowed?

Logbook lets people submit without a Hackatime link, and says so on the page. Your program decides whether to accept that.
