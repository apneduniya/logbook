A review link looks like `https://logbook.boogles.dev/r/` followed by 22 letters and numbers. Anyone with the link can read the page. To record a decision, you sign in and must be a reviewer for the submission's program.

## Read a submission

<Steps>

1. Open the link from your program's Airtable, form or inbox. It works in any browser, on a phone too.

2. If the page says **( VERIFYING )**, wait. Logbook's server is recounting every minute from the signed logs. The page reloads by itself when it's done, usually within a few minutes.

3. Read from the top: SUBMISSION (who, which days, which program), then HOURS. The rest of this guide walks through each part.

</Steps>

<Shot name="web-review-phone.png" alt="The top of the review page on a phone: the project name, submitter, dates and program, then the counted hours as the biggest figure" />

All times and days on the page are in UTC, because reviewers and submitters sit in different time zones.

## Sign in to decide

<Steps>

1. Ask the program's owner to add you as a reviewer. They need your Logbook username. You get one by signing in at [logbook.boogles.dev](https://logbook.boogles.dev) with your Hack Club account. You don't need to install anything. (Owners: see [Add reviewers](/programs/add-reviewers/).)

2. On the review page, find the DECISION panel. It's in the right-hand column on a computer. Press **Sign in**.

3. Sign in with Hack Club. You come straight back to the same review page.

</Steps>

## How to tell it worked

Once you're signed in as a reviewer for the program, the DECISION panel shows **Approve**, **Adjust** and **Reject**. On a phone, the panel moves up to sit right under the hours.

If you still see "No decision yet" and no buttons:

- You aren't a reviewer for this submission's program yet. Ask the owner to add you, then reload.
- You're the person who submitted it. Nobody can decide on their own submission.
- The page says "This submission isn't addressed to a program". Then nobody decides on it: the link is evidence only.

<Aside type="caution" title="Treat the link like a password">
Anyone with the link can read the submission. Share it only where your program keeps its records. Logbook hides these pages from search engines.
</Aside>

## Other things you might see

| The page says | What it means |
|---|---|
| There's no submission at this link | The link is wrong or cut short. Check you copied all of it. |
| The submitter withdrew this submission | There's nothing to review, and no decision can be recorded. |
| This submission failed its check | The evidence couldn't be verified, so there's nothing to review. The submitter has been told why and can submit again. |
