You decide in the DECISION panel. On a computer it's in the right-hand column and stays in view as you scroll. On a phone it sits right under the hours.

You need to be signed in as the program's owner or one of its reviewers. If you don't see the buttons, see [Open a review link](/reviewers/open-a-review-link/#how-to-tell-it-worked).

The submitter sees your decision and your reason, so write reasons for them.

## Approve

Use this when the counted hours are right.

<Steps>

1. Press **Approve**.
2. The panel asks "Approve all 12.4 h?" with the submission's counted hours. Press **Approve** again to confirm.

</Steps>

Approving takes no reason.

## Adjust

Use this to approve fewer hours than were counted.

<Steps>

1. Press **Adjust**. A short form opens in the panel.
2. In **Hours to approve**, type the hours, such as `11.5`. It must be less than the counted hours. To accept all of them, approve instead.
3. In **Reason**, say what you took off and why. For example: "Took off the 2 days with unbacked minutes."
4. Press **Adjust** to confirm, or **Cancel** to back out.

</Steps>

<Shot name="web-review-adjust.png" alt="The Adjust form open in the DECISION panel: an Hours to approve field with a hint saying less than the counted hours, a Reason box with a character count, and Adjust and Cancel buttons" />

## Reject

Use this when the hours shouldn't be approved at all.

<Steps>

1. Press **Reject**.
2. In **Reason**, say why, so the submitter can fix it.
3. Press **Reject** to confirm, or **Cancel** to back out.

</Steps>

Reasons can be up to 500 characters. The counter under the box shows how many you've used.

## How to tell it worked

The buttons are replaced by a stamp: **APPROVED**, **ADJUSTED** with the approved hours (for example **ADJUSTED 11.5 h**), or **REJECTED**. Under it are your username, the date and time in UTC, and the reason.

<Shot name="web-review-decided.png" alt="The DECISION panel after approving: an APPROVED stamp in inverted ink, with the reviewer's username and the time in UTC under it" />

The submitter sees the same stamp on their review page and in their list of submissions. Your program's inbox moves it out of **To review**.

## Change a decision

Under the stamp, press **Record a new decision** and decide again. The newest decision is the one that counts. Earlier ones are kept in Logbook's audit log. Press **Keep the current decision** to back out.

## When Logbook refuses a decision

The message appears by the button you pressed. What you typed stays.

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| This submission was checked again since you read it | Another of the submitter's computers sent its logs, and the numbers changed. Press **Reload**, read the new numbers, then decide. |
| Your sign-in ended | Press **Sign in again**, then decide. You come back to the same page. |
| A sentence about a banned or changed Hackatime account | You can't approve or adjust right now. You can still reject. See [Evidence and trust](/reviewers/evidence-and-trust/#warnings-that-change-what-you-can-do). |
| The submitter withdrew this submission | There's nothing left to decide. |

<Aside title="Once you decide, the submission is locked in">
After a decision is recorded, the submitter can't withdraw the submission, and its review link keeps working for your program and for later checks.
</Aside>
