Reviewers can open your program's inbox and approve, adjust or reject submissions. Only the app's owner can add or remove them.

Before you start, each reviewer needs a Logbook account: they sign in once at [logbook.boogles.dev](https://logbook.boogles.dev) with Hack Club. Ask them for their Logbook **username** (it's in the profile section of their **Settings**).

## Add a reviewer

<Steps>

1. Open **Apps** from the account menu and choose your app.

2. In the REVIEWERS panel, type their username under **Add a reviewer**. Capitals don't matter.

3. Choose **Add**. The field says "Added *username*. They can open this inbox now." and they appear in the list.

   <Shot name="web-app-detail.png" alt="An app's page with the REVIEWERS panel listing the owner and one reviewer, and an Add a reviewer field" />

</Steps>

An app can have up to 50 reviewers besides its owner.

## What reviewers can and can't do

- They see the app on their own **Apps** page, marked "reviewer for" you, with the inbox and the credentials panel's client id.
- They can record decisions on your program's review links. See [Decide](/reviewers/decide/).
- They can't edit the app, rotate its secret, change reviewers or delete it. Those controls are hidden for them, and the server refuses them anyway.
- They can't decide on their own submissions, even if they are a member.

Send new reviewers to the [reviewers guide](/reviewers/). It explains the review page from the top.

## Remove a reviewer

Choose **Remove** next to their name and confirm. They lose the inbox at once, and a decision they try to record after that is refused. Decisions they already recorded stay.

<Aside>
The owner can't be removed, and there's no way to hand an app to another owner yet. Pick an owner account that will stay with the program.
</Aside>

## How to tell it worked

- The reviewer is listed in the REVIEWERS panel as "added *date* by *you*".
- When they open **Apps**, your app is there, and its inbox shows your program's submissions.
- If adding fails, the field says why: an unknown username, someone who's already a member, or the 50-reviewer limit.
