Registering an app gives your program a client id and secret for OAuth, and, if you want, a place to receive submissions. It takes a few minutes. You need a Logbook account: anyone who can sign in with Hack Club can register an app.

## Fill in the form

<Steps>

1. Sign in at [logbook.boogles.dev](https://logbook.boogles.dev), open the account menu and choose **Apps**, then **Register an app**. Or go straight to [logbook.boogles.dev/apps/new](https://logbook.boogles.dev/apps/new).

2. Fill in the fields.

   - **Name**: what teens see when they sign in and when they submit, such as `Example Program`. Up to 64 characters.
   - **Slug**: what teens type to send you hours (`logbook submit … --program example-program`). 2 to 40 characters of `a-z`, `0-9` and `-`. It follows the name until you edit it. **It can't change later**, and nobody can reuse it, even after you delete the app.
   - **Homepage** (optional): where people can read about your program.
   - **Redirect URIs**: where Logbook sends people back after they allow or deny access. 1 to 5 of them. Each must be `https://`, or `http://localhost` or `http://127.0.0.1` (any port) for development. They must match what your code sends **exactly, character for character**.
   - **Take submissions**: on by default. It lists the app as a program so teens can send it their hours. Sign-in works either way.
   - **Client type**: **Confidential** if your server can keep a secret (most programs). **Public** for apps that run on people's own devices: no secret, and every sign-in must use PKCE. This can't change later.

   <Shot name="web-apps-new.png" alt="The Register an app form with Name, Slug, Homepage, Redirect URIs, a Take submissions checkbox and the Confidential or Public client type" />

3. Choose **Register app**. You land on the app's page.

4. Copy the **client secret** from the CREDENTIALS panel now, and put it in your server's secret store. Logbook keeps only a hash of it, so this is the only time you see it. The **client id** stays on the page.

   <Shot name="web-app-detail.png" alt="An app's page: FACTS with the slug and redirect URIs, CREDENTIALS with the client id and a masked client secret, the inbox and the reviewers list" />

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution">
Never put the client secret in a browser page, a mobile app or a public repo. If it leaks, choose **Rotate secret** on the app's page. The old secret stops working at once; tokens already issued keep working.
</Aside>

## Change it later

As the owner, choose **Edit** in the FACTS panel to change the name, homepage, redirect URIs or **Take submissions**. Slug and client type stay as they are.

One person can own up to 10 apps. **Delete app** stops every token and grant at once and takes the app off the submit list; submissions you already received keep its name.

## How to tell it worked

- The app shows on your **Apps** page with its slug and "yours".
- If you ticked **Take submissions**, the app's FACTS panel says "Takes submissions: people can send it their hours."
- Your client id and secret get a token. For a confidential app, this should print JSON with an `access_token`:

  ```sh
  curl -s -u "$CLIENT_ID:$CLIENT_SECRET" \
    -d grant_type=client_credentials -d scope=submissions \
    https://api.logbook.boogles.dev/oauth/token
  ```
