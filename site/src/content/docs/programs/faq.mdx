## Do teens have to stop using Hackatime?

No. When a teen who already used Hackatime installs the agent, it keeps forwarding their heartbeats to Hackatime by default, so Hackatime keeps the same data while you compare. See [Switch from Hackatime](/programs/switch-from-hackatime/).

## Why is `total_seconds` lower than on Hackatime?

On Logbook it's counted time: idle minutes are cut and a minute counts once, even across projects. Add `raw=true` for time at the keyboard, or read `raw_seconds`, which stats and project responses include next to it. What counts: [What counts](/users/what-counts/).

## Why does a teen have 0 counted seconds?

They haven't installed the agent, or it hasn't counted anything in that range. Counted time comes only from the agent. Their `raw_seconds` can still be above zero from editor heartbeats. See [Install Logbook](/users/install/).

## Why is a teen's trust level `blue` when Hackatime shows something else?

Logbook masks the level the way Hackatime does in public: `yellow` reads as `blue`. A teen who hasn't linked Hackatime on Logbook also reads as `blue`, with `logbook.trust_verified: false`.

## Can I look users up by Slack id?

Not yet. Use the numeric `id` from `authenticated/me`, or the username. To match teens with your Hackatime records, use `logbook.hackatime_id` from `authenticated/me`.

## Can I get a webhook when someone submits?

Not yet. Poll `GET /api/v1/submissions` with your app's token. See [Receive submissions](/programs/receive-submissions/#pull-them-into-your-own-system).

## Can I approve submissions through the API?

No. Decisions are recorded on the review page by a signed-in owner or reviewer. The API lets you read them.

## Can I change my app's slug?

No. The slug is fixed at registration, and a deleted app keeps it, so nobody can take a program's name. Pick it carefully.

## I lost the client secret.

Logbook keeps only a hash, so it can't show it again. Choose **Rotate secret** on the app's page and copy the new one. The old secret stops working at once; tokens you already have keep working.

## Sign-in says "This app's link is broken."

Logbook doesn't recognise the client id, or the `redirect_uri` your app sent isn't exactly one of the app's redirect URIs. Compare them character for character, including the trailing slash, and add the right one under **Edit**.

## My token calls get `401`.

Check the token goes in `Authorization: Bearer …`, not the URL. It may have expired (access tokens last 1 year, client-credentials tokens 1 hour), or the teen may have removed your app in **Settings → Apps with access**. User endpoints don't accept a client-credentials token, and the submission endpoints accept only that kind.

## Can I call the API from a browser?

No. The program API sends no CORS headers. Call it from your server.

## How many apps and reviewers can I have?

Up to 10 apps per owner, and 50 reviewers per app.
